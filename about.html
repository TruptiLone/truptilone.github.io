A little about me

From the customer's problem to a system in production.

I'm a software engineer who builds AI systems that hold up in production: retrieval pipelines, agentic workflows, and the backend and serving infrastructure underneath them. I recently finished my M.S. in Information Systems at Santa Clara University, with coursework in machine learning, deep learning and NLP.

Before grad school I spent three years building on cloud SaaS platforms in lending and enterprise operations. I worked directly with underwriters, finance and leadership to turn messy manual processes into automated workflows, integrations and CI/CD pipelines. That work taught me to start from the user's real problem, not the technology.

Lately I've been building agentic and LLM applications: a voice-driven real estate assistant with RAG, an AI operations copilot for independent cafés, and MCP-based multi-agent tools. I'm also going deeper into AI infrastructure: model serving with NVIDIA Triton, LLM inference with vLLM, and benchmarking latency and throughput.

I'm looking for a team where I can ship AI products end to end, from the customer conversation to a reliable system in production.
